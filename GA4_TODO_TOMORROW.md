# Google Analytics 4 - Reminder

**Date Created:** July 15, 2026

### 🔔 Task to complete after 24 hours:
1. Open Google Analytics: [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com/)
2. Go to **Admin** (Gear icon bottom left).
3. Under Data display, click on **Events**.
4. Look for the following events in the list:
   - `signature_created`
   - `document_uploaded`
   - `document_downloaded`
5. Click the toggle next to them to **Mark as Key Event** (previously called "Mark as Conversion").

*Note: It usually takes 24 hours for new events to move from "Realtime" to the main Events table. Once you mark them as key events, Google Analytics will stop counting these sessions as a bounce!*
